Connect to your own Leafwake server to browse audiobooks and podcasts, stream across files, keep your listening place and download supported audio for offline listening. Read supported companion PDF documents. Your media stays under your server administrator's control. No books or subscriptions are included.

Leafwake is independently maintained and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Audiobookshelf project. Compatible Audiobookshelf installations are supported within the versions and limitations recorded in the release evidence.
