# Leafwake Android listing

Name: Leafwake

Short description: Listen to your self-hosted audiobooks and podcasts.

Connect directly to your own Leafwake server and enjoy your audiobook and podcast library. Browse your books, stream audio, download for offline listening and keep listening progress across devices. Read supported PDF companion documents while listening.

Leafwake is free to download. You need a self-hosted server and your own media; no books or subscriptions are supplied. The first public Android version is a Cast-free beta. It also supports compatible Audiobookshelf servers, with the tested server versions and limitations recorded on the release page.

Leafwake is independently maintained and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Audiobookshelf project. Source code and corresponding build materials are available under GPLv3, with the applicable third-party licenses retained.

Do not advertise Chromecast, Apple Watch, Android TV or unverified reader formats for this Android beta. Store review access must use a reachable synthetic-media server. No private-library screenshots or owner credentials belong in a listing.
