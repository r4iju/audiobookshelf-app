Your library,
wherever you listen.

Leafwake serves your self-hosted library and connects your Android player directly to it. Your books and podcasts stay in your library.

Android beta in preparation

The first beta is free to download and does not include Chromecast. Your own Leafwake or compatible Audiobookshelf server is required. The app supplies no books.

Leafwake is independently maintained by Emanuel Franzen. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Audiobookshelf.

Unified server prerelease downloads, source and tested compatibility are available on the release page for updates.
