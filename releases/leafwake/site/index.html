Your library,
wherever you listen.

Audiobook Loft serves your self-hosted library and connects your Android player directly to it. Your books and podcasts stay in your library.

Android beta in preparation

The first beta is free to download and does not include Chromecast. Your own Audiobook Loft or compatible Audiobookshelf server is required. The app supplies no books.

Audiobook Loft began as an independently maintained fork of the Audiobookshelf app. Its browser and backend have been rewritten. Upstream copyright and license notices are retained. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Audiobookshelf project. Independently maintained by Emanuel Franzen.

Unified server prerelease downloads, source and tested compatibility are available on the release page for updates.
