## Legacy journeys compared

Legacy keys used by each area's screens (`components/`, `pages/`), and how many have a native text that means the same:

| Area | Legacy keys | Mapped | Unmapped |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Connection | 19 | 5 | 14 |
| Browse | 45 | 19 | 26 |
| Detail | 74 | 38 | 36 |
| Player | 32 | 15 | 17 |
| Downloads | 26 | 6 | 20 |
| PDF | 22 | 19 | 3 |
| Settings | 70 | 27 | 43 |

Every unmapped key was checked against the native English texts (`build-qa/l10n/legacy-area-keys.txt`). After the sort fix below, none is a missed equivalent. The unmapped keys fall into these groups:

- Android, Android Auto or legacy-only features with no native counterpart: local folders and storage, shake to reset, vibration, Android Auto, mp3 index seeking, lock orientation, playback method (Direct, Local, Transcode), listening history (not on legacy iOS either), old server authentication warnings, the series download sheet.
- Features the native app offers under different behavior, so the legacy wording would say something else. For example, native "Mark unfinished" has no legacy action (legacy only marked finished), and native sleep timer presets and the auto sleep window are worded differently. Native PDF "Spread" and "Two pages" are not the legacy "Layout" and "Auto". Native shows no "No Chapters" state.
- Generic controls that native leaves to the system: Ok, Back, Submit, Read more, Read less, Show all.
- Near-matches that differ in number or meaning, so they are deliberately left unmapped: Accounts and Account, Languages and Language, Titles and Title, minute and minutes. There are no exact unmapped matches (`exact-candidates.txt`, `fuzzy-candidates.txt`).
